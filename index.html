About the company

A technology company built on engineering discipline and research.

RogueByte is an independent technology company founded by Fares Sid Ahmed. We build software, POS systems, security tooling, digital forensics platforms, AI experiments, property management systems, office software and hardware diagnostics utilities — combining engineering discipline with applied research.

Our approach is simple: understand the system, document findings clearly, and turn useful ideas into working tools. Every project aims to be practical, well-engineered and honest about its maturity.
